Privacy Policy

How PTLS collects, uses and protects your information.

This is placeholder policy text for the demo site. Replace it with your reviewed legal copy before launch.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details such as name, email, phone number and company.
  • Job data including pickup and drop-off locations, load details and bids.
  • Location data from drivers while a job is active, used for live tracking.
  • Payment information processed by our payment partner.

2. How we use it

We use your information to operate the marketplace, match loads with carriers and pilot car drivers, process payments, provide support and improve the service.

3. Sharing

Job details are shared only with the parties involved in that job. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is restricted to staff who need it to support you.

5. Your choices

You can update or delete your account information at any time from your profile, or by contacting support@ptls.com.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us.
