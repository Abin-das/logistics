1. Accounts

You must provide accurate information and keep your login credentials secure. You are responsible for activity under your account.

2. Using the marketplace

  • Shippers must describe loads accurately, including dimensions and weight.

3. Payments & fees

Payment terms are agreed between parties when a bid is accepted. PTLS charges a service fee on completed jobs, shown before you confirm.

Cancellation terms are shown on each job. Repeated late cancellations may lead to account restrictions.

PTLS provides the platform connecting shippers, carriers and drivers and is not itself a carrier. Cargo liability rests with the contracted carrier.

We may update these terms and will notify you of material changes. Questions? Contact us.
