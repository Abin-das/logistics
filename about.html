About PTLS

We're building the operating system for oversize and heavy-haul logistics.

Our Story

Born on the road, built for the road.

Moving an oversize load used to mean dozens of phone calls: finding a carrier, lining up pilot cars in every state, chasing permits and waiting weeks to get paid. PTLS started as a simple way for one dispatcher to book escort drivers faster.

Today it connects shippers, trucking companies and pilot car drivers across the country on a single platform, with transparent bidding, permit-aware routing and instant payouts.
