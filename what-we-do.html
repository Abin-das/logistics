What We Do

Tools for every role in heavy-haul logistics: shippers, dispatchers and pilot car drivers.

For Shippers

Get your freight covered, fast.

Post a load and it instantly enters a bidding process with all available trucking companies. Compare offers, negotiate terms and pay when the job is complete.

  • Post loads in under two minutes
  • Compare bids by price, rating and ETA
  • Track shipments in real time
  • Pay securely on delivery
Start Shipping
For Trucking Companies & Dispatchers

Keep every truck loaded and every driver moving.

Find loads with instant quotes, manage your fleet and drivers, book pilot cars when needed and split jobs into lanes based on state permit requirements.

  • Load board with instant quoting
  • Vehicle & driver management
  • Permit-aware lane planning
  • Instant payment on completion
Start Dispatching
For Pilot Car Drivers

More escort jobs, less time on the phone.

See escort jobs near you with routes, dates and rates shown up front. Accept the ones that fit, log your miles in the app and get paid the moment the job closes.

  • Set your own day, hourly and overnight rates
  • Lead, chase and height-pole jobs
  • Built-in mileage & time tracking
  • Fast payouts, no invoicing
