How It Works

From posted load to paid job, here's what happens at every step.

Schedule a Shipment

Add pickup, drop-off, dimensions and dates for your load.

Compare Bids

Verified carriers bid; review rates and ratings, then accept.

Track the Route

Follow trucks and pilot cars live, lane by lane.

Deliver & Get Paid

Confirm delivery and payment is released instantly.

By Role

What each partner does

Shipper

Posts the load, reviews bids, chooses a carrier and releases payment on delivery.

Trucking Company

Bids on the load, assigns a driver and truck, and requests pilot cars for each permit lane.

Pilot Car Driver

Accepts escort jobs for a lane, tracks time and miles in the app, and gets paid on completion.

FAQ

Common questions

Creating an account is free. A small service fee is applied only when a job is completed and paid.

We check operating authority, insurance and certifications before anyone can bid or accept a job, and re-verify them regularly.

PTLS breaks the job into lanes based on each state's permit requirements, so you can book the right number of pilot cars for every leg.

As soon as the shipper confirms delivery, payment is released to the carrier and pilot car drivers. No invoices to chase.
